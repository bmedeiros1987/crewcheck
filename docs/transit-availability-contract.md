## Pure contract

shared/transitAvailability.mjs and .d.mts keep schedule available/unavailable/unknown separate from operational normal/disrupted/suspended/unknown. Closed service or suspension blocks; disruption requires alternative; absent, invalid, stale or mismatched evidence stays unconfirmed. The UI only consumes the no-evidence result, and explicitly says source not connected/update unavailable. No green operator status is inferred from a successful itinerary.

Every trip carries an offset-qualified departureAt, IANA operator timezone and identified ordered boarding/alighting legs. Each leg retains explicit serviceDate. GTFS seconds are measured from local noon minus 12 hours of that service date, including DST transitions, without modulo 86400: GTFS 24:30 remains 88200 of the preceding service day, regardless of browser timezone. Calendar dates/holiday exceptions must be resolved and confirmed by an eventual adapter; the contract does not guess holidays or weekly schedules.

Schedule evidence requires matching date/timezone/line/station, validity covering the whole leg, explicit calendar confirmation and serviceRuns. Each window has opening, exclusive closing and last boarding cutoff. Every transfer leg must have coverage; arriving at closing or missing the last connection makes the route unavailable. Station/line IDs must come from actual itinerary, not airport inference.

Operational evidence requires scoped coverage, explicit active validity and fresh fetchedAt. sourceUpdatedAt has a declared semantic: last-change may be old if a fresh official response explicitly still reports that status; refresh must itself be fresh. Active disruptions/suspensions overlapping any leg outrank normal. A normal observation now does not confirm a later departure. All provenance is returned independently; renderer must not label raw/invalid provenance as confirmed.

## Independent review corrections

Scope is a discriminated union: explicit `kind: line` with no station field, or `kind: station` with nonempty string line/station IDs. Empty/null/number/boolean IDs are rejected; malformed entries invalidate the evidence rather than expanding it to all stations. Source URLs require HTTP(S) without embedded credentials; only valid evidence provenance is returned.

Every leg identifies origin, destination, intermediate stations and `stationCoverageComplete: true`. Missing/incomplete identity stays unknown. Schedule/normal require all relevant stations or explicit line-wide coverage. An alert at any endpoint/intermediate station overrides normal origin; every transfer leg remains required.

Hero, departure time, status class, KPI, estimate source/leaveLabel and card use transitDeparturePresentation. Unknown transit displays “Horário a confirmar”/“A confirmar”, never ready/protected departure; durations do not populate driving cache. Transit messages cannot claim automatic departure or Telegram monitoring. Actual AirportDeparture/estimate are rendered in tests across route states and positioning, with positive driving control.

Unverified transit duration never falls back to driving cache/default minutes. Only an ok, valid, current-mode transit itinerary provides travelMinutes/travelLabel. Pending/error/stale/drive-mode responses return travelMinutes null and “Duração não confirmada”. leaveDate/leadMinutes are null while departure recommendation is unconfirmed; valid itinerary duration alone does not permit a departure recommendation. Actual estimator fields and rendered KPI are tested alongside mode switches and stale driving responses.
