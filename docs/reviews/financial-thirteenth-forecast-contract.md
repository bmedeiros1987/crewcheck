## Small implemented contract

The optional Salary panel accepts an explicitly reviewed annual year, averaging
period, avos and remuneration. It does not infer avos from flight hours, roster
days or employment dates without a service/absence producer. Unknown avos are
pending, distinct from an explicit reviewed zero. A full12-avo equivalent is
multiplied by avos/12 and rounded half-up once in integer centavos. An already
proportional annual amount is never multiplied by avos again. A positive already-proportional amount with zero avos, or a mean ending
after the annual year, is contradictory and cannot produce a total. Deadlines
before the1965 payment-law version are not modeled. There is no vacation
third. Partial reviewed remuneration remains a subtotal; full gross requires an
explicit completeness review. The program does not certify a user's document.

Retentions, personal consignments and advance offsets are separate reviewed cash
items. Duplicate item identities invalidate net while preserving valid gross.
None reduce remuneration or create a tax base; no monthly IRRF estimator is used.
No first-parcel amount is guessed. Net stays unknown until all deductions/offsets
are explicitly reviewed, including zeros. Nothing propagates from vacation or
monthly settings. MCP is not a global default, average component or recurring rule.

Owner, session/token, year, plan/source revision and averaging period are separate
fences. Auth changes discard the ephemeral scenario, includingA→B→A; reload and
remount also start empty. No local financial setting, ledger credit or server data
is written. No message, consent prompt, financial-channel activation or extra
provider query is introduced. The same privacy control and scoped form styling
are reused; the vacation component/source is otherwise preserved.

## Account boundary under deferred rendering

Drafts are fenced by the existing auth generation as well as owner/token. A rapid A→B→A return, same-owner token replacement/return, logout/relogin or auth storage event cannot revive the previous scenario while React resets are deferred. Reset callbacks capture identity at the event; stale callbacks and edits cannot lend their old draft to the new context. Nothing is persisted. The browser regression deliberately defers resets using React transitions while forcing higher-priority renders, and checks dates/year, money and confirmations in both real components. It does not use sleeps or relax the original account-return assertions.
