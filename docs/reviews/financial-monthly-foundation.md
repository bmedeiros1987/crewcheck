# Monthly finance foundation — separate from visual preview

This step is based on main 2286c88, not on #1054. It does not change pages, the active compensation engine, statement import confirmation, #1018, messaging, collection or training. No new UI is rendered and no real account is populated. The visual proposal remains subject to Bruno's feedback.

## Competences and credits

The new ACT context extends financialPayrollPeriods without changing the existing reviewed-cycle API. Operational month M, tariff month M+1 and expected credit month M+2 are distinct. Fixed month M+1 requires independent explicit company guidance; clause 3.2.8 is not its source. The ACT source gives a fifth-business-day deadline, not an actual day-five deposit. No exact date or settlement is inferred. Both [cabin ACT, clause 3.2.8, p.15](https://aeronautas.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20251208-ACT-Aeronautas-Comissarios-25-27_v9-site.pdf) and [pilot ACT, clause 3.2.8, p.18](https://aeronautas.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20251209-ACT-Aeronautas-Pilotos-25-27_v13-site.pdf) were consulted.

The existing financialReadModel now also validates full-month payroll and vacation net-credit aggregates. They reuse owner isolation, revisions, rejection of ambiguous duplicates, unknown amounts and forecast/statement/settlement separation. Forecasts cannot invent a payroll credit day. Actual late credit can be recorded in its actual month while retaining the original expected month. Negative or gross/informational/compensation amounts cannot masquerade as a net credit. Vacation receipt net stays separate from monthly payroll net; a service day in the next month is not a second credit. Producers must use canonical payment IDs, not a new credit for each page or different header. Rubric parsing/reconciliation remains a later step; no personal PDF is bundled here.

## Versioned reference catalogue

ACT floor-with-CO references use the printed floor and a single 2.09% adjustment, derived from half the [IBGE November 2025 INPC twelve-month 4.18%](https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Precos_Indices_de_Precos_ao_Consumidor/IPCA/Fasciculo_Indicadores_IBGE/2025/ipca-inpc_202511caderno.pdf). Public floor tables and notes in clauses 2.3.1/2.3.2 were checked for cabin, copilot and captain, during/after probation. Applicability is explicitly TAM, selected function/stage and 2025-12 through 2026-11. Overall ACT duration through 2027 does not extend this economic version past the next indexation.

The cabin general salary clause 2.2.1 mentions a different index reference interval from its floor notes; this catalogue specifically follows the floor notes and does not apply that derivation to individual salaries. References retain printed value, derivation, includes-CO flag, source URLs and version. They are not guaranteed personal base pay and are not auto-adopted. Chief supplements and personal deductions remain unconfigured. Owner/month-scoped personal values (including explicit zero or unknown) precede reviewed statements; discrepancies remain visible, not overwritten or indexed a second time. Out-of-period and other-company lookups return no reference. Private observed tariffs are not generalized into this public catalogue.

## 2026 tax estimates

[INSS employee table](https://www.gov.br/inss/pt-br/direitos-e-deveres/inscricao-e-contribuicao/tabela-de-contribuicao-mensal) is selected by contribution competence. The pure estimator requires explicit single-employment applicability, applies progressive bands/cap and truncates every bracket to cents before summing, per [eSocial MOS S-1.3, S-5001 §2.13, page 384](https://www.gov.br/esocial/pt-br/documentacao-tecnica/manuais/mos-s-1-3-consolidada-ate-a-no-s-1-3-11-2026-com-marcacoes-retificada.pdf#page=384) and S-1200 §5.2, page 134. Calculation metadata reports truncate_each_bracket and reference version v2. Multiple employments and special payrolls require review.

[Receita Federal monthly IRRF 2026](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/meu-imposto-de-renda/tabelas/2026) is selected by payment month. The API requires an ordinary-monthly context; it does not silently handle 13th/vacation special taxation. Legal deductions mean the complete known deduction, including applicable INSS once. Legal and simplified alternatives are never added together. Best-known mode chooses one alternative. Additional reduction uses taxable income before deductions, not the resulting tax base; it is capped by ordinary tax and cannot become negative. Null income/deductions needed by the chosen mode stay unknown. No current personal deductions or active salary calculations are replaced.

No recurring propagation, migration, historic rewrite, removal of other settings or deletion of documents occurs. This manual entry API cannot forge the existing importer's reviewed provenance or auto-adopt a catalogue reference. This local configuration resource is not server profile synchronization and has no UI integration in this step; individual import/configuration UI and actual filling remain pending. No new access or credentials were requested.

## Recovery: ACT validity and source separation

The ACT variable-cycle helper now rejects cycles unless operational, tariff/payroll and expected-credit months are all wholly inside 2025-12-01 through 2027-11-30. This conservative projection boundary implements duration and clause 6.4 without assuming ultratividade. December 2025 is the first operational month accepted; September 2027 is the last complete cycle. October/November 2027 cross-boundary projections return null pending a separately reviewed applicable policy; this does not decide or erase accrued payment rights. 2024-09 and 2028-09 also return null.

Clause 3.2.8 supports only the variable cycle. Fixed competence/source are null by default and require independent explicit user-reported company guidance as the third argument. The original company-guidance API remains unchanged. Boundary tests cover both validity edges, cross-year cycles, missing/wrong policies and independent fixed provenance. No personal values, authenticated account or external financial service is used.
