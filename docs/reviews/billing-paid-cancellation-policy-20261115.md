# Paid-period cancellation policy — isolated preparation, HOLD

Product decision: stop renewal on cancellation, retain access through an established paid period, and reconcile refunds separately. This prospective product choice does not establish historical accepted terms or payment coverage.

`createPaidCancellationPolicy` accepts only a trusted injected server coverage reader and clock. It requires exact owner/public identity, provider, subscription and payment binding; an identified/versioned authoritative source; actual paid bounds; fresh evidence; confirmed stopped renewal and no unresolved refund. Trial/grace/status, expiry alone, a received payment date or the historical 31/366-day calculation do not qualify. Unknown, conflicting or unavailable evidence returns reconciliation required with no access mutation. Different existing/paid ends require separate reconciliation; cancellation cannot silently grant or extend access. Exact expiry returns paid-period-ended. The trusted clock is read again after the asynchronous read, so evidence cannot outlive its validity while awaiting it.

This module has no route, network transport, database writer, token persistence or timer. **No real authoritative coverage adapter is configured or implemented.** The existing cancellation helper and Asaas cancellation webhook retain their previous behavior and inconsistency; this isolated policy does not fix the live flow. Google authoritative expiry alone is also insufficient to prove paid rather than trial coverage. Provider rules and effective commercial terms must be reconciled before operational wiring.

The selected input is copied before awaiting evidence. This protects object binding, not session revocation or A→B→A. A future caller must revalidate live account authority, current coverage/refund state and the period inside the same entitlement transaction. A returned decision is neither a grant capability nor permission to write outside that transaction.

Synthetic regression covers 26 cases: Asaas and Google positive contracts, expiry, account/provider/subscription/payment mismatch, non-paid/legacy/unversioned evidence, pending/completed/partial/unknown refund, unconfirmed renewal, stale/future/malformed evidence, conflicting period, future paid start, unavailable reader, mutated input identity and clock advancement during await. No real provider, user receipt, payment, cancellation, schema or storage is touched. Independent read-only review found the stale-clock issue; the revised implementation adds post-await clock validation and two boundary counterexamples. Full Android/browser, actual SQL integration and remote CI are not established by this pure policy regression.

Remaining gates: authoritative payment-specific commercial coverage source, actual session/transaction integration, cancellation webhook/direct-route consistency, refund/chargeback policy, exact final review/CI. Production and PR #1342 remain HOLD. No changes to existing terms, production, retention or automatic recovery.
