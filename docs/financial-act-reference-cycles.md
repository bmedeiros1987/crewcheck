The private import preview counts individually dated occurrences by rubric, page and line, compares their integer-cent sum against the single deposit already validated by the existing payment-date parser, and blocks reviewed application on mismatch/conflict. A repeated footer does not create a second payment. Historical advance rows outside the work period do not become current tariff observations. Coffee and taxi remain distinct even on the same date/flight/amount. Undated or unsupported layouts stay unconfirmed; absence never certifies zero. International floors are not promoted to enterprise tariffs and overnight admission is retained.
